Why VaakSetu?

Built for the places where cloud-first tools fall short.

01

Ease of use

A multilingual application UI and single-page workflow keep the complete translation journey easy to navigate.

02

Works without internet

Local AI models make translation possible in bandwidth-constrained and air-gapped environments.

Multimodal by design
