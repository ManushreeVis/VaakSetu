01 / The problem
The last mile is not one problem

Field knowledge gets stuck between language, media, and connectivity.

01

Work happens in many formats

Notes, voice recordings, videos, PDFs, and spreadsheets arrive together. Teams need one workflow to understand all of them.

02

Meaning changes across languages

Marathi, Hindi, and English are not interchangeable when terminology matters. Consistency needs a shared glossary.

03

Cloud access cannot be assumed

Rural and operational environments need reliable, private processing without data egress or a fragile network dependency.

02 / The solution
One workspace, many ways to understand

From raw media to ready-to-use knowledge.

01 / Aa

Translate with control

Text and batch workflows connect source and target language with glossary-backed terminology.

  • TXT, MD, CSV, JSON, DOCX, PDF
  • Auto-select or choose a model
  • History, ratings, and downloads
02 / ◉

Make media searchable

Speech becomes transcript, translated segments, subtitles, and voice output in a single pipeline.

  • Whisper transcription
  • SRT / VTT and TTS output
  • FFmpeg extraction and burn-in
03 / ?

Ask the document

Upload knowledge, chat with it in your language, generate summaries, and keep the result auditable.

  • Document-aware Q&A
  • Voice in and voice out
  • Summaries and job archive
