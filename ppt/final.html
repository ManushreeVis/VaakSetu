Problem • 01
Why This Matters

Field teams are blocked by language, connectivity, and fragmented AI workflows.

⚠

Language barriers in the field

People working in rural and operational environments often need to read, listen, translate, and summarize content across Marathi, Hindi and English — without specialist technical support.

📡

Connectivity is unreliable

Cloud-first translation services fail or become costly in bandwidth-constrained environments. The codebase explicitly prioritizes offline, on-premises execution for privacy and sovereignty.

🧩

Workflow fragmentation

Teams juggle text translation, media transcription, glossary enforcement, summaries, and document Q&A in separate tools. VaakSetu consolidates them into one operational workflow.

Solution • 02
What the product does

A single multilingual AI hub for translation, media intelligence, and knowledge work.

📝

Text & Batch Translation

Translates plain text, documents, and batch files across English, Hindi and Marathi with terminology controls.

  • IndicTrans2-powered translation
  • TXT, MD, CSV, JSON, DOCX, PDF inputs
  • Glossary-driven consistency
🎙️

Media Translation

Captures speech from audio and video, translates it, and generates subtitles or voice output.

  • Whisper ASR for speech-to-text
  • FFmpeg preprocessing + timing
  • SRT/VTT generation and TTS output
💬

Document Q&A & Summaries

Brings uploaded knowledge into an interactive conversational workflow for field research and decision support.

  • Document chat sessions
  • Voice-enabled assistant replies
  • Summary generation and audit history
